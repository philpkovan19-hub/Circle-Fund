CircleFund brings the old wisdom of neighbourhood savings circles on-chain. A small group. A shared pot. Everyone gets a turn.

Form a circle

Pick a size, a monthly amount, and how long each round lasts. Friends join the seats.

2

Contribute each round

Every member pitches in the same amount. Rounds tick over automatically.

3

Take your turn

Each round, one member takes the whole pot. When everyone's had a turn, the circle is complete.
